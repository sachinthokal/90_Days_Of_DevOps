## My Understanding of DevOps & Cloud Engineering
```
DevOps is a mindset and a set of practices that bridges the gap between Development and Operations teams.  
Its goal is to reduce time-to-market by enabling automation, faster CI/CD and efficient infra.

```

```
My long-term interest lies in infrastructure ownership, system reliability, and operational excellence.
```
